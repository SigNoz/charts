The CRDs came from the chart's crds/ directory. Helm applies those once and
never touches them again, so `helm upgrade` will NOT bring them up to date.
Before upgrading to a chart version with a new appVersion, apply them yourself:

  helm pull signoz/signoz-operator --version <version> --untar
  kubectl apply --server-side -f signoz-operator/crds/
